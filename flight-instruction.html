Flight training

From first hover to career pilot.

At Alpine Aviation, we provide comprehensive flight instruction for both rotor and fixed-wing aircraft. Whether you're flying for fun or planning a career in aviation, our experienced instructors and personalized programs will help you reach your goals.

🚁 Helicopter flight training

100%success rate — every student who has trained with Alpine Aviation has graduated and is now working in the commercial aviation industry.

Our proven training process, real-world experience, and customized instruction give students the confidence and skill to succeed. We offer helicopter training for:

  • Private Pilot License (PPL-H) — Learn to fly for personal enjoyment. Take family, friends, and coworkers flying while acting as Pilot in Command.
  • Commercial Pilot Certificate (CPL-H) — Take your skills to the next level (and make money doing it). A commercial license is required to be paid for flying and opens doors to professional aviation jobs.
  • Certified Flight Instructor (CFI-H) — Teach others to fly helicopters while building valuable experience and flight time.

✈️ Fixed-wing (airplane) flight training

We now offer fixed-wing flight instruction, using the same student-focused, high-quality training approach that made our helicopter program so successful. Whether you're brand new to flying or looking to become a professional pilot, our fixed-wing program meets you where you are. We offer fixed-wing instruction for:

  • Private Pilot License (PPL-A) — Fly for personal use — perfect for recreational pilots or future aircraft owners.
  • Commercial Pilot Certificate (CPL-A) — Get paid to fly. Launch your career in commercial aviation, cargo transport, or flight services.
  • Certified Flight Instructor (CFI-A) — Teach others to fly airplanes while continuing to build your hours and experience.
