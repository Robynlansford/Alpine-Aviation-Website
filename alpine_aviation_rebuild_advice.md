# Alpine Aviation Website Rebuild — Strategic Advice

## What You Have (Current State)

Based on analysis, **flyalpineaviation.com** is a multi-location helicopter flight school and commercial services operator with:

- **Locations:** Mountain Home/Oasis, Idaho (primary), Grass Valley, California, and Lewiston, Idaho
- **Core offerings:** Helicopter & fixed-wing flight instruction, commercial helicopter services, helicopter tours, livestock management/pest control, student housing, financial partnerships (Stratus Financial)
- **Key claim:** 100% graduate success rate — every student excels and enters the commercial field
- **Likely tech:** WordPress-based (URL structure, author pages, permalink patterns)
- **Competitors:** Silverhawk Aviation Academy, Cascade Air, regional Idaho flight schools

---

## Critical Recommendations

### 1. Structure & Information Architecture — Fix the Multi-Location Problem

Your biggest UX issue is that **three locations (Idaho, California, Lewiston) are collapsed into one site with no clear separation**. Prospects need to know *which location* they're dealing with immediately.

**Proposed sitemap:**

```
Home
├── Flight Training
│   ├── Helicopter Programs (PPL, CPL, Instrument, CFI)
│   ├── Fixed-Wing Programs
│   └── Discovery Flights ← primary conversion point
├── Commercial Services
│   ├── Helicopter Tours
│   ├── Agriculture & Ranching
│   └── Pest Control
├── Student Housing
├── Financing
├── About
│   ├── Mountain Home, ID
│   ├── Grass Valley, CA
│   └── Lewiston, ID
├── Testimonials / Career Outcomes
└── Contact
```

### 2. Conversion Architecture — Every Page Needs a Clear CTA

Helicopter schools have a **high-consideration purchase journey**. Prospects research credibility, aircraft access, instructor qualifications, and career outcomes before booking.

- **Primary CTA on every page:** "Book a Discovery Flight" or "Schedule a Consultation"
- **Secondary CTA:** "Call Now" (click-to-call on mobile)
- **Placement:** Above the fold, sticky nav, and end of every content section
- **Lead capture:** Add a simple inquiry form that captures name, phone, email, and interest area — don't force a phone call as the only option

### 3. Social Proof — Leverage Your 100% Success Rate Aggressively

You claim a 100% graduate success rate with students working commercially. That's a massive differentiator but it's buried on a separate testimonials page.

- Feature graduate outcomes prominently on the homepage hero
- Add photos of graduates in cockpits, with their new employers, or in uniform
- Include video testimonials (Facebook videos suggest you already have some)
- Create a "Where Our Graduates Work" section with employer logos

### 4. Visual Strategy — Aviation Demands Video

Helicopter flight training is inherently visual. Static images won't convey the experience.

- **Hero section:** Full-width or full-screen video of Idaho mountain flying — that's your unique selling point (rugged terrain, pristine lakes, deep canyons)
- **Program pages:** Short embedded videos showing actual training flights
- **Drone/GoPro footage:** Student POV shots from inside the cockpit
- **Consistent brand colors:** Aviation blue/white palette with high contrast for readability

### 5. SEO — Own the Local Terms

Flight school SEO is highly local. You need to dominate:

- "helicopter flight school Idaho"
- "helicopter pilot training Mountain Home"
- "helicopter flight school Grass Valley California"
- "helicopter tours Idaho"
- "helicopter pest control services"

**Action items:**

- Create location-specific landing pages (not just an About section)
- Each location page needs its own schema markup, Google Business Profile integration, and local keywords
- Publish a blog with topics like "Cost of Helicopter Training in Idaho 2026," "Career Paths After Your CPL(H)," etc.

### 6. Technical Stack — Consider a Modern Approach

If you're on WordPress (which the URL structure suggests), that's fine, but consider:

- **CMS:** WordPress with a lightweight page builder (Elementor Pro, Bricks, or Gutenberg blocks) — avoid bloated themes
- **Hosting:** Managed WordPress hosting (WP Engine, Kinsta) for speed and reliability
- **Performance:** Target Core Web Vitals scores above 90 — slow sites kill flight school conversions
- **Mobile-first:** 60%+ of your traffic will be mobile (prospects researching on phones)
- **Booking system:** Integrate a scheduling tool (Calendly, Acuity, or a custom booking widget) for discovery flights

### 7. Content Priorities

| Priority | Page | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| 🔴 | Homepage hero with video | First impression, immediate CTA |
| 🔴 | Flight Training overview | Your bread and butter |
| 🔴 | Discovery Flight booking page | Primary lead gen |
| 🟡 | Commercial Services breakdown | Secondary revenue stream |
| 🟡 | Student Housing | Unique differentiator |
| 🟡 | Financing/Partnerships | Removes a major barrier |
| 🟢 | Where Graduates Work | Social proof hub |
| 🟢 | Blog/Resources | SEO content engine |

### 8. What to Avoid

- **Don't** put all three locations on one page without clear separation
- **Don't** hide your 100% success rate — it belongs above the fold
- **Don't** use stock aviation imagery — use real footage from your fleet and training area
- **Don't** make prospects dig for pricing — at least provide ranges (even "starting at $X/hr")
- **Don't** neglect the commercial services audience — they're a separate buyer persona from student pilots

---

## Bottom Line

Your business has strong fundamentals — multiple revenue streams, real geographic advantage (Idaho terrain is perfect for training), solid graduate outcomes, and unique offerings like student housing. The rebuild should focus on **clear conversion paths**, **location-specific navigation**, **video-first visuals**, and **aggressive social proof**. Treat discovery flights as your product — everything on the site should push toward booking one.
