About us

A tight-knit school, not a pilot factory.

Who we are

Alpine Aviation is a professional flight school and commercial helicopter operator based in Oasis, Idaho, with additional operations in Grass Valley, California and Lewiston, Idaho. For over a decade we've combined real-world aviation experience with personalized instruction and top-tier aerial services.

How we teach

We provide comprehensive flight training in both helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, in a focused, one-on-one learning environment — hands-on instruction from high-time, experienced instructors actively working in the aviation industry.

What makes us different

We aren't a pilot factory — we're a tight-knit school focused on quality over quantity. Our mission is to create confident, capable, and career-ready pilots.

  • ✅ Dual-Specialty Training — We offer both rotorcraft and fixed-wing instruction, giving students a broader range of skills and job opportunities.
  • ✅ 100% Career Placement Rate — Every past graduate from our program has gone on to work in commercial aviation.
  • ✅ Real-World Commercial Exposure — Students train alongside instructors involved in active commercial work, gaining valuable on-the-job experience.
  • ✅ Small Class Sizes — With limited enrollment, students get the personal attention they need to succeed, and get their license in no time.

If you're serious about becoming a professional pilot, we're currently interviewing students for both helicopter and fixed-wing programs. Let's talk about your aviation goals!

Commercial helicopter operations: "We come to you"

Alpine Aviation provides a wide range of commercial helicopter services throughout Idaho and the surrounding region. Our specialties include:

  • 🐄 Cattle Mustering
  • 🌾 Crop Pollination & Drying
  • 🌄 Helicopter Tours
  • 🛠 Utility & Survey Work
  • 🦊 Predator Control

Thanks to our custom-built helicopter trailer, we can bring our aircraft directly to your location — cutting out costly ferry time and offering lower rates than the competition. This mobility allows us to deliver high-quality commercial services wherever and whenever you need them.

Our pilots have thousands of hours of experience in real-world commercial operations and are equipped to handle challenging jobs with safety and professionalism.
