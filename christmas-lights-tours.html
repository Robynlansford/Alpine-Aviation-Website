Two ways to fly it

We offer two distinct helicopter tours to experience the festive holiday lights in Boise.

Private Downtown Boise Christmas Light Tour

An exclusive, personalized tour of Boise's holiday lights from the sky with an hour-long flight.

Downtown Boise Christmas Lights — Group Tour

A group tour providing a magical aerial view of the city's Christmas decorations, coordinated out of Jackson Jet Center. If there are fewer than 4 passengers, the total for the Treasure Valley Light Tour is $1,600.00*.
